About Adversarial Delivery

We deliver software you can actually ship, built through an adversarial multi-agent loop and deterministic safety gates.

Why “adversarial”

Every unit of work goes to an independent reviewer whose job is to break it. The reviewer is a separate AI agent that sees only the artifacts. Work goes back until it passes.

Known-bad patterns are refused by deterministic gates, so catching them does not depend on someone noticing.

Why we state our limits

A limit you learn about after launch is a limit you paid for. So we say up front that a Gate Pass is not an audit, that we review contract designs rather than Solidity source, and that our reviewer is not a person.
